* splat is "Sound Pressure Level Annotation Tool" and is a piece of locally installed C# software on some of our NPS windows machines that is used for annotating noise events on an acoustic record. The primary use case is to look at 1s sampling rate sound pressure level data and visually delineate noise events based on a spectrogram visualization. However, it also has logic for extracting the full audio clip from the same timeframe so it's how we often discover new audio clips because we see something interesting in the 1s spectrogram.
